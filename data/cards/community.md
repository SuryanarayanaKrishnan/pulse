# UAE card stacking and gotchas (community and blog findings)

Collected 2026-11-24. Reddit was not reachable, so there are no direct r/UAECreditCards quotes. Everything below is either an official term (marked OFFICIAL) or secondary/community-sourced and unverified. Do not treat unverified items as data for the reward engine.

## Tabby / Tamara repayment and wallet top-ups

- SIB Cashback card, Tabby repayment (community-sourced, unverified). Customer reports say Tabby repayments post under MCC 5999 (miscellaneous retail) and earn only the 0.5% tier, even when paid online or through Apple Pay or Samsung Wallet. A wallet does not override the exclusion. A past 10% repayment does not guarantee the next one. Source: https://kredit.ae/blog/sib-tabby-cashback.md
- Fee math (same source, aggregator blog): Tabby can charge up to about 1% to repay by credit card. On AED 3,000 that is AED 30 against AED 300 cashback at 10% (net AED 270), but only AED 15 cashback at 0.5% (net loss AED 15). Check the posted MCC and the amount credited before repeating it.
- Tamara: no source found with per-bank behaviour. Treat as unknown.
- SIB advice from the blog: wait for the repayment to post, note the date and merchant description, ask SIB which MCC and category were applied, and keep the case reference. Tabby repayments share the same AED 300 monthly cap with other online spend.
- Wio Credit pays other-bank card bills at 0.5% (needs Wio Plus plan and AED 5,000 other eligible spend that cycle; total Wio cashback cap AED 2,500 a month; bill-pay cashback limited to 0.5% of the Wio credit limit a month). Wallet top-ups, cash advances and quasi-cash are excluded by Wio's own terms. Source: https://kredit.ae/blog/how-to-get-extra-cashback-pairing-uae-credit-cards (blog dated July 2025, check current Wio terms).
- Wallet top-ups in general: most issuers classify them as quasi-cash or exclude them. Not individually verified per bank here.

## MCCs banks commonly exclude or downgrade

- OFFICIAL (SIB cashback T&C, https://sib.ae/en/Cashback-TCs): low-rate MCCs are utility and telecom 4900, 4813, 4814; government 9399, 9402; education 8299, 8220, 8211; supermarkets 5411. Utility bills paid through SIB's own online or mobile banking earn nothing. Balance transfers, Easy Cash, fees, takaful charges, and cash withdrawals earn nothing.
- OFFICIAL (DIB cashback cards, https://dib.ae/personal/cards/consumer-platinum-card): category cashback only after a minimum monthly retail spend (AED 4,000 Platinum, AED 3,000 Reward); caps were cut for supermarkets and school fees from 10 Oct 2026.
- OFFICIAL: DIB Skywards terms (via aggregator) say DIB-platform utility and government bill payments earn no miles.
- Blog (unverified): hotel restaurants can post as hotels (MCC 7011) so dining bonuses do not apply; snacks at petrol stations can post as convenience or grocery rather than fuel; food-delivery apps sometimes use generic codes; lounge extras may post as airport services; DEWA, SEWA and Salik pay lower because bank fees on them are capped. Source: https://kredit.ae/blog/mcc-code-issues.md
- Fuel, rent, education, government and wallet top-ups are the usual excluded or reduced categories across UAE cards. Always check each card's `excluded_categories` and rule `conditions`.

## SIB 10% online card tricks and limits

- OFFICIAL: 10% applies to online, saved-card app/website payments, Apple Pay and Samsung Wallet. Cap AED 300 per calendar month. Utilities, telecom, government, supermarkets and education are excluded from the 10% tier. No minimum spend. International retail 2%, domestic 1%. Cashback expires after 36 months, redemption minimum AED 100, cannot be used to pay the card bill. Annual fee waived from year two with AED 10,000 retail spend. Sources: https://sib.ae/en/cashback-card-faqs and https://sib.ae/en/Cashback-TCs
- Reaching the cap takes about AED 3,000 of qualifying spend, so it is a niche pick for one monthly big online purchase.
- Returns reduce the month's cashback and can claw back more than was earned if the cap had applied (OFFICIAL T&C).

## Apple Pay / Samsung Pay bonuses

- SIB: wallet payments count toward the 10% tier (OFFICIAL).
- DIB SHAMS and UAB cards: UAB's welcome (Entertainer VIP Key) needs one mobile-wallet payment plus one taxi or food-delivery app payment within 30 days of receiving the card (aggregator, kredit.ae UAB pages).
- No other bank wallet-bonus claim was confirmed from a primary source.

## Fee-waiver tricks (from official or aggregator pages)

- SIB cashback: AED 10,000 retail spend a year waives the fee (OFFICIAL).
- Ajman LIFE World: AED 630 fee waived by AED 5,000 retail spend within 60 days of issue (aggregator).
- EI Switch Cashback: free first year, then waived at AED 30,000 annual retail spend (OFFICIAL EI page).
- DIB SHAMS Infinite: free first year (approved by 31 Dec 2026), then AED 60,000 annual spend.
- ADIB SHARE Platinum and Infinite: waived from year two at AED 45,000 or 75,000 total spend, or AED 15,000 or 30,000 non-AED spend.
- Ruya Cashback: free first year, then AED 30,000 spend.
- Annual fees on many Islamic cards are quoted with VAT; check which figure a source uses.

## Cap-stacking pairings (blog-sourced, unverified)

- Wio Credit as a bill-pay card for other banks' card balances (0.5%) combined with high-rate cards elsewhere. Needs repayment in full to avoid interest.
- SIB cashback card for one large monthly online purchase, up to AED 300 back.
- EI Switch Cashback in Lifestyle mode for fuel (8%, AED 100 cap) and supermarkets/dining/education (4% each, AED 200 cap), paired with a flat-rate card for everything else. Needs AED 2,500 monthly spend (AED 4,000 per cycle from 10 Jan 2027).
- DIB Consumer cards: AED 3,000 to 4,000 monthly spend threshold applies to the whole cycle, so pair with a card that handles exempt categories.
- Spread categories across cards by cap: each category cap is usually per statement cycle, per card.

## Other gotchas

- Many Skywards, Etihad Guest and SmartMiles rates are quoted per USD 1, not per AED.
- Co-brand welcome offers (ADIB Marriott, EI Amazon, DIB SHAMS) have 2026 expiry dates and need fee payment or spend windows.
- Citi UAE retail cards, Smiles (SIB) cards and Invest Bank cards are of uncertain current availability.
